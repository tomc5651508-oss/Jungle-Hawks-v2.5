# Impact FX - fire, hits and smoke

*Ships inside Pingu's Jungle Hawks v2.5, and as a standalone mod for the stock game (Pingu's Impact FX 2.5 - vanilla).*

Impact FX replaces the stock hit, fire and smoke effects with staged, calibre-scaled ones.

## What it changes

- **Staged fires** - a hit fuel tank smokes, then catches, then burns; engine fires grow from a smoke trail to flame
- **Hits by calibre** - rifle-calibre strikes spark, .50 cal and 20 mm flash and tear, HE shells burst; armour hits
  look different from skin hits
- **Smoke you can see from the next valley** - long, wind-blown trails from burning aircraft and wrecks
- **Muzzle effects** - blast, glow and smoke on every fixed gun
- **Crashes and ground fires** - fireballs, burning wrecks and smoke columns that stay

## How it works

A GB Mod Loader plugin (`bin\game\gbml\impactfx.dll`) plus effect files in `data\`. Every texture and particle file the
effects use is either in the mod or in the stock game. Settings: `bin\game\gbml\impactfx.ini`.
